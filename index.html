About

My path into data science started with a foundation in Industrial Engineering. During my B.Sc. at Iran University of Science and Technology — where I graduated 6th in a cohort of 75 — I developed a deep appreciation for systems thinking, optimisation, and quantitative methods.

That grounding led me into hands-on data work: first as a Project Management Officer at a gas turbine research centre, then as a BI Junior Specialist at Pars System Energy, where I built Power BI dashboards and SQL pipelines that turned raw operational data into decisions. Working close to real business problems made the gap between static reports and intelligent, adaptive systems impossible to ignore.

In October 2024 I moved to Germany to pursue an M.Sc. in Management & Data Science at Leuphana University (current GPA 1.4), while working as a Data Analytics & AI working student at PMI Advisory GmbH. Today I focus on LLM pipelines, RAG architectures, and the practical side of bringing AI into organisations — not just building models, but making them useful.

Knowledge Graph Question Answering (KGQA)

Live

A hybrid NLP pipeline that answers natural-language questions over the DBpedia knowledge graph — chaining entity linking (DBpedia Spotlight), 1-hop relation filtering, LLM-based SPARQL generation (Llama 3.3 70B), and live query execution with automatic retry on failed or empty results. Research

Replication and extension of Baio & Blangiardo (2010): Bayesian hierarchical models for predicting football results, fitted with MCMC (NUTS) on Italian Serie A data. Extends the paper with team-specific home advantage and a covariate model decomposing home advantage into stadium quality, travel distance, and fatigue. Course project for Probabilistic Modelling at Leuphana University.
