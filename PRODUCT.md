# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers at German and international companies screening for data / analytics / AI roles. They arrive from a CV, a LinkedIn profile, or a job application, and they skim: the site gets 30–90 seconds to establish credibility before they move on. A secondary audience is university staff and potential research collaborators at Leuphana.

## Product Purpose

A personal CV/portfolio site (farmand-bt.github.io) that presents Farmand Bazdiditehrani's experience, education, projects, skills, and certifications. Success means a recruiter leaves with the impression of a credible, rigorous junior candidate who already ships working AI products — and then downloads the CV or connects on LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A master's student who already builds and deploys working LLM and data products — not just coursework. Honest junior positioning: working toward data science, data engineering, and AI engineering roles, never claiming a title he doesn't hold yet.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: a paired goal — **Download CV** and **LinkedIn**. These are the two channels recruiters actually use; both must be easy to find and act on.
- Secondary CTA: email (the direct-contact fallback for anyone ready to talk).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "A master's student who already builds and deploys working LLM/data products — not just coursework."
- Belief ladder: (1) this person is real and professionally presented → (2) the academic record is strong (GPA 1.4, ranked 6th of 75) → (3) the projects are real, dated, and live — I can click and use them → (4) the positioning is honest, so the claims are trustworthy → (5) worth a CV download / LinkedIn connect.
- Proof on hand: three live Streamlit demos with public GitHub repos, verifiable certification links (Credly, Skilljar, Sulitest), named employers and universities, and the downloadable CV at `assets/CV.pdf`.

## Brand Personality

Polished, credible, quietly confident. Refined editorial voice: precise, warm, unhurried — the tone of someone rigorous enough to let facts speak. Never salesy, never inflated.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-templated portfolio sites: the interchangeable dark-hero-with-gradient-text developer portfolio a recruiter has seen a hundred times.
- Inflated-title personal branding ("Data Scientist & AI Engineer" as a self-claimed identity) — the site must read as honest.
- Cluttered CV dumps: dense walls of bullets with no hierarchy that punish skimming.

## Design Principles

- **Skimmable first.** A recruiter in 30 seconds must catch: who, what level, proof, how to act. Hierarchy serves the skim path; depth is available but never in the way.
- **Proof over claims.** Every strong statement is adjacent to something verifiable — a live demo, a repo, a grade, a certificate link.
- **Honest positioning.** Student working toward roles, stated plainly. Credibility compounds; inflation destroys it.
- **Editorial restraint as differentiation.** In a sea of templated developer portfolios, calm typographic confidence is the memorable move.
- **Improve, don't rebuild.** The site works and its identity is established; changes refine what's shipping.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 body-text contrast, full keyboard operability, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation. Both light and dark themes must independently meet the bar.
